Sprint retro with the engineering team to discuss what went well and what slowed us down this sprint.

---

Attendees: Sam (Eng Lead), Alex, Riya, Tom

Sam opened by asking the team to rate the sprint from 1-5. Average landed
around a 3 — decent, not great. The main pain point raised was the number
of unplanned interrupts from the support queue, which ate into feature
work for both Alex and Riya.

Tom suggested rotating a dedicated "support day" each sprint so interrupts
don't spread across everyone's focus time. The team agreed this was worth
trying for one sprint before deciding whether to keep it.

On the positive side, the new CI pipeline cut average build time from nine
minutes to just under four, which the whole team called out as a big
quality-of-life win.

Action items:
- Sam: set up the rotating support-day schedule starting next sprint
- Tom: document the CI pipeline changes in the eng wiki
- Alex: file the two remaining support tickets that got deprioritized this sprint
